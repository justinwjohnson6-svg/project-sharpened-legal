Privacy Policy

Project Sharpened Studio. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Who this covers

Project Sharpened Studio is a private desktop tool used only by its owner for accounts the owner controls. It does not collect information from the public or from other TikTok users' accounts through TikTok login.

Information the app uses

  • Basic TikTok profile information for the connected account, such as display name and avatar.
  • TikTok sign-in tokens that let the app act for the connected account after the owner approves it.
  • Videos, captions, and notes the owner chooses to prepare or send.

How it is used

This information is used only to show which account is connected, prepare the owner's content, and send drafts to that TikTok account when the owner chooses.

Where it is stored

App data and sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's computer. The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Services that receive data

  • TikTok receives sign-in requests and videos the owner chooses to send.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model and may receive text the owner chooses to process, such as transcripts, captions, and notes.

Removing access and data

The owner can remove the app's access in TikTok settings and delete its local data from the computer.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated on this page. For questions, message @project_dayone on TikTok.
